Why Ringana

A company built on one uncomfortable ingredient label.

I've spent my career explaining what happens inside a woman's body during hormonal transitions — puberty, postpartum, perimenopause. Ringana's founding story is the same instinct, aimed at what goes on the body instead.

1996

The toothpaste moment

In Hartberg, Austria, Andreas Wilfinger and Ulla Wannemacher read the ingredient list on a toothpaste their young son had brought home — and didn't like what they found in a product made for a child. Rather than accept it, they built the alternative.

2013

The Frischewerk

Ringana opens its own "fresh factory," bringing formulation and production fully in-house — no outsourcing, no third-party fillers.

2021

The campus

A new headquarters opens in St. Johann in der Haide, Austria — one of the country's most ambitious sustainable builds, powered by geothermal and solar energy.

2026

America

Ringana invests $85 million in its first U.S. headquarters and production facility in Roanoke, Virginia — so products are made fresh on American soil, for American customers.

Next

Your chapter

The American story is just beginning. The people who step in before launch help write what it becomes.

"What goes on your body and in your body should need no apology." — the conviction Ringana was founded on, and one I recognized the moment I first read their ingredient philosophy.
Where It's Made

Straight from the campus — never a distant factory.

Every Ringana product starts and ends in the same place: their own production campus in St. Johann in der Haide, Austria — not a co-packer, not a contract manufacturer three steps removed from the formula. That distinction matters to me professionally, not just as a slogan. When a company controls its own production, an ingredient list stops being a marketing promise and starts being something you can actually trace.

The campus itself runs on geothermal and solar energy, which fits the same logic: fresh production only makes sense if the process behind it doesn't cut corners either. In 2026, that same standard is coming stateside, with Ringana's first U.S. facility opening in Roanoke, Virginia — made fresh, closer to home, for the people I'm building this with here.

Who's behind this

Hi, I'm Luisa.

I spent thirteen years as a special education teacher in Germany before I stepped fully into the work I do now: helping women understand what's actually happening in their bodies during perimenopause, and helping their daughters understand puberty before it becomes confusing or frightening. Precision matters to me — I don't say something is true unless I can point to the science behind it.

I found Ringana during my second parental leave, half-looking for a way to build something of my own around my family rather than in spite of it. What kept me was the same thing that keeps me in my educational work: nothing oversold, nothing dumbed down, just a genuinely well-made product I could stand behind. Nine years and a team of over 500 women later, it's still the part of my week I look forward to most.

America is Ringana's next chapter — and I'd love to help the people my husband and I know here be part of writing it.
