Who's behind this

Hi, I'm Luisa.

I spent thirteen years as a special education teacher before stepping fully into the work that carries me today: helping women understand what's actually happening in their bodies during menopause, and helping girls understand puberty before it becomes confusing. Precision matters to me in both.

I found Ringana during my second parental leave, looking for something of my own to build around my family rather than in spite of it. What kept me was the same thing that keeps me in my educational work: nothing oversold, nothing dumbed down — just a product I can genuinely stand behind. Nine years and a team of over 500 women later, it's still the part of my week I look forward to most.

America is Ringana's next chapter, and I'd love to help the people here be part of shaping it.

The Short Version

A brand that started at a kitchen table.

I spend my working days explaining what's changing in a woman's body during hormonal transitions. Ringana's founding story follows the same instinct — just aimed at what goes on the skin instead.

1996

The Spark

In Hartberg, Austria, Andreas Wilfinger and Ulla Wannemacher read the ingredient list on a toothpaste their son brought home from daycare — and weren't thrilled with what they found. Rather than accept it, they built their own alternative.

2013

In-House Production

Ringana opens its own manufacturing facility, bringing development and production fully in-house — no contract manufacturers, no outside suppliers for the formulas.

2021

New Headquarters

A new campus opens in St. Johann in der Haide, Austria, powered by geothermal and solar energy.

2026

America

Ringana invests $85 million in its first U.S. facility and production site in Roanoke, Virginia — so products are made fresh right here.

Where It Comes From

Everything happens in one place.

Every Ringana product is developed, made, and shipped from the same campus in St. Johann in der Haide — no contract manufacturer, no interchangeable supplier somewhere in between. That matters to me beyond a slogan: when a company controls its own production, an ingredient list becomes a verifiable fact instead of a marketing promise.

The campus runs on geothermal and solar power — the same standard carries through the production itself. In 2026, that same bar comes to America, with the first U.S. site in Roanoke, Virginia, closer to the people I'm building this with.

The Products

Four categories, one shared standard.

Ringana isn't a single hero product — it's a considered system for skin and body, all held to the same bar: made fresh, plant-based at full potency, free of what doesn't need to be there.
